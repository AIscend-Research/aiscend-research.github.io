[Re] A Novel CNN Ablation Reveals Limited Fourier Inductive Bias in LiteFNO: A Reproducibility Study

Jacob Goldstein*, Kristen Zhou*, Wenhao Lu*, David J. Zhang, Spursh Deshpande, and Hyunjun Yi *Equal contribution.

We study LiteFNO, a lightweight Fourier Neural Operator for time-dependent PDEs, in two ways: as a reproducibility study, since no public implementation of the described architecture was available, and as a novel ablation that adds a parameter-matched low-rank CNN the original paper never includes, isolating the Fourier inductive bias. Our central question is whether the Fourier machinery earns its complexity.
