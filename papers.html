Contaminated Ancestors: Seven Ways Our Own Evaluation Protocol Nearly Told Us the Wrong Thing

Catalogs seven evaluation-protocol failure modes, from ancestral sequence leakage to split contamination, that each produced a spurious result before being caught.

Accepted into NeurIPS Creative AI

Restored Lines: A Model Finds the Poet’s Hardest Line Breaks and Declines to Write Them

Uses token-level predictive entropy to locate the least predictable line breaks in a poem and has the model abstain from generating text at those positions.

Accepted into NeurIPS Creative AI

The Dead Ear: A Language Model’s Inherited Accent Is an Archive, Not an Author

Measures the metrical and lexical priors a language model inherits from its pretraining corpus and argues the resulting prosody is corpus retrieval rather than authorship.
